Payment Terms & Conditions

  1. Fee payment is accepted in installments as per the schedule mentioned above.
  2. A late payment penalty of ₹50 per day will be charged for payments made after the due date.
  3. Fee once paid is non-refundable and non-transferable under any circumstances.
  4. All payments should be made in favor of "Khatamari Shishu Bikash Academy".
  5. Please preserve the payment receipt for future reference and verification.
  6. For any payment-related queries, please contact the accounts department during working hours.
  7. The school reserves the right to revise the fee structure with prior notice to the parents/guardians.
