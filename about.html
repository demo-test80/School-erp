Welcome to Khatamari Shishu Bikash Academy

Established in 2005, Khatamari Shishu Bikash Academy has been a beacon of quality education in the region, nurturing young minds and shaping futures for over 18 years. Our commitment to academic excellence, character development, and holistic education has made us one of the most trusted educational institutions in the area.

Our school is built on the foundation of providing a nurturing environment where every child can discover their unique potential. We believe in fostering a love for learning that goes beyond textbooks, encouraging curiosity, creativity, and critical thinking in our students.

With a team of dedicated educators, state-of-the-art facilities, and a curriculum that balances academic rigor with co-curricular activities, we prepare our students to face the challenges of the 21st century with confidence and competence.

Our History

The journey of Khatamari Shishu Bikash Academy began with a vision to provide quality education to the children of our community. What started as a small initiative with just a handful of students has now grown into a premier educational institution with a reputation for excellence.

Over the years, we have consistently evolved our teaching methodologies, upgraded our infrastructure, and expanded our academic programs to meet the changing needs of education. Our alumni, who are now successful professionals across various fields, stand as a testament to the strong foundation we provide.

As we look to the future, we remain committed to our core values while embracing innovation in education to prepare our students for a rapidly changing world.

Our Vision & Mission

Guiding principles that shape our educational approach

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution that nurtures responsible, compassionate, and globally competitive individuals who contribute positively to society. We envision a learning community where every student discovers their potential and develops the skills and values necessary to thrive in an ever-changing world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a stimulating learning environment that encourages students to achieve their full potential academically, socially, and emotionally. We are committed to:

  • Delivering a comprehensive and balanced curriculum that meets international standards
  • Fostering a culture of innovation, creativity, and critical thinking
  • Nurturing values of integrity, respect, and social responsibility
  • Providing opportunities for holistic development through sports, arts, and community service
  • Building strong partnerships with parents and the community to support student success
