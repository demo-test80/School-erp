Admission Process

Step 1: Submit Application
Fill out the online application form with all required details and documents.
Step 2: Application Review
Our admission committee will review your application within 3-5 working days.
Step 3: Assessment & Interview
Shortlisted candidates will be called for an assessment test and interview.
Step 4: Admission Offer
Successful candidates will receive an admission offer via email/phone.
Step 5: Fee Payment
Complete the admission by paying the required fees within the given deadline.
